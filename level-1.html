Level 1 of 5 · Ad Hoc

Ad Hoc · ORBIT: Opportunistic

Individuals discover value on their own

AI assistants are sanctioned and individuals use them ad hoc. Value is real but personal, invisible, and unrepeatable. The organization has licenses and a policy, and little else.

Entry gate
First sanctioned license issued and an acceptable-use policy published.The insight: Prohibition ends. Risk is managed, not avoided.
Start measuring now
Telemetry belongs at Level 1, not Level 4. GitHub’s Engineering System Success Playbook makes baselining the first step of any improvement, including AI adoption: gather quantitative and qualitative baseline data before you change anything, because you cannot show a gain without a before. GitHub’s own AI adoption playbook warns that companies invest millions in licenses and watch adoption stall near 20 percent; the ones who can course-correct are the ones who were measuring. Level 4 is where measurement runs the portfolio; day one is when it should start recording. Turn usage analytics and telemetry on with the first sanctioned license, and capture 4 to 12 weeks of pre-AI delivery baselines while you still can, since the before-state is unrecoverable once adoption spreads. See the metrics appendix for what to capture.Sources: GitHub ESSP (2025); GitHub AI adoption playbook; DORA ROI of AI-assisted Software Development (2026).

Requirements

People & Skills

  • Individual engineers experiment with sanctioned assistants
  • Champions self-identify; no formal enablement program exists
  • Skill lives in individuals and leaves with them

Process & Workflow

  • AI use is invisible to the defined SDLC; results vary by person
  • No named workflows exist; each use is improvised
  • Wins are anecdotal and cannot be handed to a teammate

Platform & Tooling

  • Sanctioned licenses replace personal accounts
  • No shared configuration; every seat is configured from scratch
  • An approved-tool list exists and is enforced at procurement

Governance & Risk

  • An acceptable-use policy is published and acknowledged
  • Data-handling rules define what may and may not enter a model
  • Shadow AI is actively migrated onto sanctioned tools

Measurement

  • Seat counts and login activity are the only numbers
  • No baselines exist; before-and-after is a feeling
  • Nobody can say which teams get value or why

Exit gate
First named workflow with its configuration committed to version control, reproduced end to end by a second person.The insight: AI use becomes an artifact, not a habit.
