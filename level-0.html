Level 0 of 5 · No AI Use

No AI Use · ORBIT: Grounded

The floor, not a maturity level

No AI is in use anywhere in the organization. Sometimes this is enforced policy, sometimes inertia. Either way, the organization has no data, no skills, and no position on AI in the SDLC.

Requirements

People & Skills

  • No sanctioned AI usage or training exists
  • Curiosity is present but has no approved outlet
  • Any AI skill in the building is invisible to the organization

Process & Workflow

  • The SDLC has no AI touchpoints, sanctioned or otherwise
  • Policy or inertia blocks experimentation
  • Competitive exposure is accumulating silently

Platform & Tooling

  • No approved AI tooling of any kind
  • Shadow AI may already exist on personal accounts
  • No technical controls exist because officially there is nothing to control

Governance & Risk

  • No acceptable-use policy exists
  • Data-handling rules for AI are undefined
  • The biggest unmanaged risk is the unsanctioned use nobody can see

Measurement

  • Nothing is measured because officially nothing is happening
  • No baseline exists for later comparison
  • Anecdote is the only available signal

Exit gate
First sanctioned license issued and an acceptable-use policy published.The insight: Prohibition ends. Risk is managed, not avoided.
