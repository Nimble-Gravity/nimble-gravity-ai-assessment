Field kit

Gauging where a client is

Two instruments, one model. The placement instrument answers “what level are they?” in a 45-minute conversation. The deep audit answers “what exactly is missing?” once an engagement is underway.

1. The placement instrument

  • Fifteen ladder questions: three facets per dimension, each showing six descriptions side by side, one per level. Pick the description you can evidence today.
  • The five gates are embedded as ordinary factual questions. A hidden map scores every answer against level and dimension.
  • Placed level is the lower of the gate ceiling and the evidence ceiling (60 percent of a level’s questions, consecutively). The gap between the two is the diagnosis: practices outrunning gates is a governance engagement; gates outrunning practice is an adoption engagement.

2. The deep audit

  • Seventy-five auditable statements, fifteen per level, scored 0-2 with evidence. Run it for the levels the placement pointed at, not all five.
  • Attainment requires at least 80 percent, the entry gate, and every lower level. The dimension profile shows where the organization is lopsided.

Facilitation notes

  • Score with the client in the room, not from a survey. The disagreements between their leads are the real findings.
  • Ask for artifacts, not opinions: show me the repo config, the checkpoint definition, the dashboard, the retired workflow.
  • Ask the questions conversationally in topic order; never reveal which level a question maps to. The placed level lands harder when the client recognizes every answer as their own.
