Level 5 of 5 · Optimizing

Optimizing · ORBIT: Transformative

The SDLC is designed around agents

The process assumes agents rather than accommodating them. Specs are the source of truth, the backlog is agent-ready by default, and humans set intent and arbitrate exceptions. The economics show up externally in how work is priced and sold.

Entry gate
The SDLC is redesigned assuming agents, and AI-native economics appear in pricing or SoWs.The insight: The process is built for agents; humans work by exception.

Requirements

People & Skills

  • Humans set intent, design specs, and arbitrate exceptions
  • New roles exist: agent operations, spec engineering, verification design
  • Learning loops run continuously; every engagement updates the playbook

Process & Workflow

  • Specs are first-class artifacts and the source of truth for delivery
  • The backlog is agent-ready by default; human-only work is the exception
  • Review is exception-driven, triggered by signals rather than scheduled

Platform & Tooling

  • Multi-agent orchestration runs with atomic claim locks and no race conditions
  • Agent pipelines are self-serve for any team inside governed boundaries
  • Verification is continuous, not a stage

Governance & Risk

  • Policy is enforced as code inside the pipeline itself
  • Economic guardrails cap spend and route exceptions automatically
  • Clients see transparent, contract-level terms for AI-delivered work

Measurement

  • Economics are outcome-based and visible in pricing
  • Leading indicators trigger action before lagging ones confirm it
  • Running experiments is standard operating procedure, with a budget

Terms that matter here

spec-driven developmentagent-ready backlogpolicy-as-codeexception-driven reviewoutcome-based pricing

There is no exit gate. Level 5 is not a finish line; it is an operating condition maintained by the learning loop itself.
